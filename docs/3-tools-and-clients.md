## How I choose an approach

The agent should apply this decision order before assigning tools:

1. **Does the product need exact detail?** Accurate shape, label, or material, or the client
   wants a reusable asset → build in 3D (Blender).
2. **Does the client have usable product photos?** → composite and finish in Photoshop, with AI
   for backgrounds and scenes.
3. **Is it a scene, mood, or background with no product accuracy requirement?** → AI-led,
   finished in Photoshop.
4. **Does the client's team need to edit it later?** → build in Canva or Adobe Express.

Default assumption: most projects are 2D plus AI. 3D is added deliberately, not by habit.

## Client industries

What each type of client cares about, and what tends to work.

### Food and beverage
Wants freshness and appetite appeal. Bright clean lighting, condensation, crumbs, steam,
visible texture. Color often comes from the food itself. Strongest fit in my portfolio.

### Home and lifestyle (candles, mugs, bottles, decor)
Wants mood and warmth. Low-key or golden-hour lighting, props like linen, wood, and dried
flowers, shallow depth. Sells a feeling more than a spec.

### Fashion and accessories
Wants texture and elegance. Detail shots paired with on-model or in-context images, neutral
backdrops, editorial layouts.

### Beauty and fragrance
Wants premium minimalism. Single focal point, shaped light, restrained palette, generous
negative space.

### Interiors and real estate
Wants space and light. Wide framing, daylight through windows, styled but livable rooms.
Newest area for me.

### Games and 3D assets
Wants engine-ready, consistent, modular assets. Prior experience, not a current focus.

## What I need from a client before starting

The agent should check this list is complete before moving past the brief stage:
- Product photos from several angles, and dimensions if 3D is involved
- Logo files and brand colors, plus brand guidelines if they exist
- Where the visuals will be used, and which platforms
- Deadline and budget range
- Reference images of visuals they like
- Any copy or claims that must appear in the design
